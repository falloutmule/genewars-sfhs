# Creature death and carried material

The numeric death core follows the literal5CDB0 order:5CDC0 first installs
state24, then5CDC4 calls5CC60/5C920. Cleanup therefore sees a dead carrier.
Material36 is retired through the real shared-effect service; carrier67 changes
to68 through the existing drop path. Missing cleanup bindings still reject the
whole staged callback.

The previous port released the old action before installing death. A living
material36 carrier correctly retains its attachment during ordinary action
release, so that ordering made natural death fail its remaining-attachment
guard. Installing death first fixes the source family rather than discarding
the attachment flag or inventing a successful retirement.

The literal death core also retains association kinds79/82 and the former
action's plant/peer reservation afterimages. State24 is already present when
5CC60 selects its action-specific branch. An earlier explicit release by a
damage caller may have cleared those reservations; this death entry does not
repeat the former action's cleanup. Local target cleanup, death flags, timer,
motion and energy writes remain in their source order. Corpses stay allocated
and spatially linked until actual retirement.

The separate AI death tail is now bound at the successful callback boundary.

player byte2 is nonzero.35F00 decrements an active matching group's signed
count, clears control bit2 on that branch, then always clears the group link
and masks the control flags to their low three bits. It neither retires the
corpse nor creates a replacement. The auxiliary save/carry projection follows
the resulting control bytes. Natural aging and lethal damage use this same
service; a late failed callback consumes no death receipt.

new death events and performs no sweep or retrospective correction.
