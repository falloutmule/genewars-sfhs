# Botanist campaign adapter checks

`tests/botanist-adapter.test.ts` covers the real campaign adapter and shared
services with synthetic content only. A synthetic retained progress1 player
admits the category1 blueprint through the actual roster and native specialist
factory. The fixture establishes readiness and placement explicitly to isolate
the adapter. It does not claim a landing playthrough, original map equivalence,
UI browser validation or mission acceptance.

The ten focused cases cover:

- Actual category1 native/player/binding identity through a completed planting
  callback, first-free plant creation, credit1 and credit8. They check plant and
  owner counts, exact retained resource cost, point credits, two actual60
  particles, native links and the complete fifteen-draw caller/factory sequence.
- Real7/60/61 factories and ascending scheduler callbacks through retirement.
  Retained fixed fractions survive publication; shared occupancy, spatial links
  and serialized count3554 agree after retirement. Factory and callback RNG are
  checked separately.
- A full200-slot synthetic pool. Code7 and equal-priority60/61 attempts fail
  without a factory draw; credit/debit caller bursts retain their three draws
  per attempt. Replacing a weaker7 carrier clears a held actor reference before
  the same slot is reused and consumes exactly one successful factory draw.
- Actual plant damage and6C6B0 ignition with stable held native/player/binding
  identities. Immediate lethal retirement clears target references, plant count,
  owner5FA and spatial membership before reuse.
- Mid-work save/load followed by planting and scheduled effects, comparing the
  complete world checksum after each continuation. A separate ordinary queued
  command survives save/load and is consumed by the real campaign callback.
- Command boundary rejection for another role, an unready actor, a dying actor,
  missing selector and unknown plant research. All three original modes are
  admitted when their own prerequisites are present; knowledge is required only
  for selected planting.
- Late failure after attempted ignition, audio, score debit and RNG. An invalid
  code61 profile causes the outer callback transaction to roll back every world
  root and retain the caller's original object identities.

The host keeps `plant.native` in place during ignition and mirrors shared
count3554 immediately on publication/retirement. These invariants are exercised
through the real adapter, cleanup, effect scheduler and persistence APIs rather
than substituted service stubs. The source control family and closed numeric
comparisons are documented separately in `BOTANIST.md`.
