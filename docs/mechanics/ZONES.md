## Scheduling and credit

The unsigned countdown at prefix`355C` and an empty zone list return before other admissions. Zones run in ascending index order, then source native slots in ascending order. A nonpositive remaining quota or signed occupancy byte18 skips a zone. This phase runs before actors and creature motion, so it observes their positions at the start of the frame.

Selectors8..12 credit gene categories0..4 on frames divisible by64. Each active living creature passes its type1 mortality virtual (`state<24`) and the separate frozen-state15 exclusion. Distance uses the source signed15-bit wrapped XY difference and an inclusive squared radius. Matching primary genes request16; matching secondary genes request8 against the _updated_ remaining quota. The final AL controls the optional trail: a secondary mismatch retains the primary result, while a secondary call returning zero suppresses it. Selector7 requests10 for every eligible creature, and separately requests8 for plants on `(frame+16)%64==0`. Selector13 visits owner partitions and building slots on `(frame+32)%64==0`: player byte607 must be nonzero, building selector7 must have flags49 masks1 and4 set and40 clear, and an in-radius result requests64. Plant/building trails require the actual source112D14 scratch yaw as an explicit context input.

`792C0` caps the requested contribution by remaining quota, then divides neutral owner4's contribution by four. It subtracts that result from the zone once. Owned credit goes to its participating owner, and other participating activated players (`+49D` unsigned nonzero) lose a quarter of that credit from approval, bounded by the negative unsigned limit at prefix`1C78`. A selected opponent also receives the original warning request70. Neutral credit rewards every participating player. Score at player`+0D` and approval at`+11` use wrapping32-bit addition; approval is capped by twice the unsigned limit at prefix`1C7C`. A positive capped neutral request that rounds to zero still invokes feedback. Mules remain in the zone and can contribute again on later scan frames; the zone callback does not consume or retire them.

## Factories and lifecycle

Every `80780` reward emits the real audio57/`0x39` request and then `max(1,credit>>3)&255` score-marker iterations. Each iteration consumes three `6D500` journal`F6` draws, commits one60/`0x3C` factory, and consumes its journal`229` delay draw before the next iteration. Speed is`64*(10+draw%10)`, pitch depends on the subject's relative-height128 boundary, and yaw is`16*(draw%128)`. Type1's physical/relative getters read39 and39−17; plant and building getters bind ground17 and relative0. Marker owner inherits the credited subject, including neutral4.

The subsequent47/`0x2F` trail consumes its own ordered yaw/pitch draws, plus an owner draw for neutral subjects, then its actual factory delay draw. Source factory failure consumes no delay draw. Both selectors preserve native counter30, fraction67/68 and untouched child73/payload76. Pointer/resource bytes31..38 remain null. New zone effects are unflagged and therefore participate in the ordinary effect primary pass later in the same frame.

Allocation uses the lowest free slot. A full pool scans source priorities in ascending order, retaining the first strictly highest unflagged candidate above the requested priority; equal priorities do not replace one another. Known descriptor priorities are admitted for the existing source effect families. Other occupied selectors require an explicit descriptor-priority provider. Replacement requires the real reference-cleanup callback before the slot identity is reused. A pool with no suitable candidate returns a source factory failure rather than inventing a free slot.

`stepZoneEffectRecord(native,47|60,terrain,{tables,bounce,profile})` is the shared scheduler port. It returns a complete numeric afterimage, ordered terrain sample points and a retirement request while retaining live native kind5. It implements constant XY integration with retained fractions, gravity acceleration already written by the factory, first terrain-impact normal bounce, source angle/speed recalculation and the grounded branch's _second_ quantized-velocity sample. These selectors' virtual70/90 responses are false, so source obstacle/water callbacks are skipped. Their actual EFF47/EFF60 profiles each have six zero-hold frames and flags0; signed counter clamping, timer decrement and terminal-frame retirement use the existing source animation helper. They have no damping or callback RNG. A positive impact requiring the unbound virtual34 damage branch rejects explicitly. `stepZoneEffect` additionally stages spatial movement, source effect count, retirement and required cleanup. `createZoneScoreMarker` exposes one fixed score-marker iteration for conformance/shared hosts.

## Exhaustion and required host ports

After a zone callback, remaining quota0 clears original quota10. Original quota0 increments prefix`34B9`, emits its original exhausted-label request, and follows signed next-zone byte5. A valid next zone with nonzero original quota restores its remaining quota, runs activating`79D50`, then calls the required family8 visitor factory port in mode2 at terrain+32000 before emitting the activation label. The old zone then runs deactivating`79D50`; existing active mode2 visitors whose byte58 identifies it receive departure byte57=1. This order is preserved even for overlapping zones and later indices in the same pass.

`79D50` walks its original wrapped vertex rectangle in row order, relights each vertex through `8C290`, applies the inner/outer radius lighting and reveal bytes when activating, marks the four linked cells dirty, and refreshes plant/building shade through their original flags15 gate. Ordered viewport invalidation points are returned for the renderer. World/UI reference cleanup, unknown full-pool descriptor priorities, source112D14 scratch yaw when used, and family8 creation remain explicit required host ports; absent ports reject the whole staged transaction. The zone module does not create substitutes for those families or decide mission victory. The existing outcome family consumes the resulting score, quotas and completed-zone count.
